Welcome to No. 18 Corn Mill, a wonderful top (second) floor 1 bedroom apartment boasting a sunny facing balcony with views across the City, very generous accommodation, ready to walk into and the benefit of a designated underground car parking space.

O'Connor Estate Agents are delighted to present this stunning bright property to the market which presents itself in pristine condition. The apartment extends to approx. 45sq.m. (484sq.ft.) and offers generous bright living space, ample storage and a large double bedroom. There is also access to a large Residents Roof Top Garden with amazing vistas of Croke Park and the City.

The accommodation briefly comprises of a large Entrance Hall with ample storage, a bright open plan living / dining room with a large sunny balcony and adjoining a modern built-in kitchen. The Bedroom is a large double with built in wardrobes and a main bathroom.

The Corn Mill development is ideally situated within walking distance from the City Centre and Drumcondra village is a short stroll away offering an abundance of shops, bars, cafes and restaurants. The development is located just minutes from Croke Park.

**TRANSPORT** - Drumcondra Train Station is only a few minutes walk away and Clontarf Dart Station is also within easy reach. There are numerous bus routes in the immediate locality, Dublin City Centre is within walking distance and the M50 and M1 are easily accessible offering excellent transport links to the rest of the county and country. Dublin Airport is also within close proximity.

**AMENITIES** - Conveniently located off Clonliffe Road there are a number of shops, bars and restaurants in the busy thoroughfare of Drumcondra and Fairview. For the sports enthusiast, Croke Park is your neighbour and there are numerous gyms, football clubs and sports grounds nearby.

Viewing is Highly Recommended.
